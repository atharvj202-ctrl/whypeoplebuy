Questions

Honest answers. Some of them slightly too honest.

Do you only work with brands in the UAE?

No. Zoom works in every time zone, and I've checked. I'm based in Dubai and work with brands in the UAE, India and anywhere else with decent Wi-Fi.

Can you make our content go viral?

Sure, right after I make it rain on command. What I can do is make people trust you, which is less exciting at parties but much better for revenue. Viral is a lottery ticket. Trust is a salary.

Do you create the content too, or just the strategy?

Strategy is the main course, but I don't leave you to cook it. For founder personal branding on Instagram, I write the reel scripts and captions. You just show up and look like you woke up that insightful. For shoots and design, I brief your team, so nobody “gets creative” with the plan.

Our product is great. Why isn't it selling?

Congratulations, so is everyone else's. Literally every competitor's website says the same thing. Customers can't tell you apart, so they pick the cheapest or the loudest. The audit finds the one thing only you can say.

Can't we just post more?

You can. Posting more of what isn't working is a bold strategy, and I admire the commitment. Or we could fix what you're saying first, then post.

How do payments work?

50% upfront, 50% on delivery. I love brands, but I love paid invoices slightly more.

What if I'm not sure which package I need?

Relax, nobody is. That's why the call is free. Thirty minutes, no slides, no “let's circle back”. Most of the time I'll tell you to start with the audit, because I'd rather you see how I think before you spend real money.
